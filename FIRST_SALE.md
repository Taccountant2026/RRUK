# RRUK: quickest route to the first paid sale

Store: https://retroreplay.uk
Owner area: https://retroreplay.uk/admin

The domain, separate live RRUK merchant, final-domain live webhook and the owner's branding/fulfilment declarations are already saved. Do not create another account, key or domain connection. The 65 sourcing entries are live and accepting free requests. No confirmed sale products have yet been supplied.

1. **Prepare one item.** Open https://retroreplay.uk/admin#products, choose its catalogue entry and use Prepare sale item. Add its actual photos, condition and checks, exact contents, compatibility, available quantity, final GBP price, tracked UK delivery charge and transit estimate. Save a draft until the details are confirmed. Publish when it can be fulfilled. The same catalogue URL becomes its sale page.
2. **Finish the final-domain test.** Open https://retroreplay.uk/admin#payments and use Run £1 test payment. This uses the saved test merchant without changing live checkout settings. Use Stripe test details: card 4242 4242 4242 4242, expiry 12/34, CVC 123 and fictional test customer/address details. Check that the return is on retroreplay.uk, the order is a verified paid TEST order and the final-domain signed test delivery appears after refreshing. No real card charge is needed. Prior refund, decline, cancellation, expiry and duplicate-event results are retained; any delayed payment methods offered still need their applicable provider tests.
3. **Open live checkout when the applicable checks are complete.** In Payments, retain the saved Live mode and owner declarations, review the merchant and use Enable live checkout. The server checks the merchant and its signed live-delivery evidence again. Do not switch the public store to test mode to run step 2.
4. **Send the ready item link.** For a matching customer request, Requests → Draft purchase invitation opens your email app with actual item and delivery totals plus the purchase link. Review and send it yourself. The listing is public and stock is checked again at Checkout; the email does not reserve it. If no request exists, share the public product page yourself.
5. **Fulfil verified paid live orders.** Check Orders, download the order/terms copy, include it with the parcel, pack the agreed item, record tracked delivery and email the customer. Never dispatch a test, unpaid, processing or review order.

The new owner tools have automated coverage. Their deployment does not itself perform the final external test, supply product photos, establish stock or open sales. Full browser/keyboard/screen-reader QA remains unavailable in this managed session.
