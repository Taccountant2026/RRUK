Policies preserve cancellation rights, statutory faulty-goods remedies and the additional six-month console warranty. They do not impose automatic restocking fees, a blanket no-returns rule, an arbitrary liability cap, a void-if-opened rule, or a compulsory refund-only/repair-only remedy contrary to law.

The store does not claim ADR membership, independent accessibility certification, manufacturer affiliation, blanket electrical certification, legal sign-off or complete legal protection. Every real listing must be checked for provenance, safety, included parts, region/compatibility, age restrictions and any specific obligations before publication. Do not publish age-restricted products until a suitable lawful age-check process is in place. Northern Ireland or other product-specific requirements must be assessed for the actual stock during catalogue research.

Privacy wording describes the implemented cart storage, hosted checkout, order records and owner authentication. Verify vendor/subprocessor arrangements and retention operations as the business grows. The ICO notes that relevant guidance is being reviewed following the Data (Use and Access) Act.
